# Contradiction Search

For every strong hypothesis, deliberately search for contradictory evidence following the investigation strategy.

## HYP-001: Target and Account A have a recurring association

**Search for contradictory evidence:**

- Evidence showing no recurring interaction beyond the initial follow
- Different contexts in which Account A appears (different communities, events)
- Conflicting identity clues (Account A may be a different person than assumed)
- Evidence suggesting Account A has a different primary association
- Observations that explain the follow pattern more simply (e.g., Account A is a public figure in Target's niche, and the follow is merely topic-based)

**Current status:** No contradictory evidence observed yet.  
**Effect on hypothesis:** Without contradiction, HYP-001 remains INVESTIGATING with MODERATE confidence.

---

## HYP-002: Target and Account B have a relationship through shared community or interests

**Search for contradictory evidence:**

- No direct interactions found between Target and Account B
- Account B's content does not share themes or interests with Target's content
- Account B is connected to Target only through the recommendation mechanism, with no other ties
- Different follower communities (Target's followers do not overlap with Account B's followers)

**Current status:** No contradictory evidence observed yet; only the recommendation signal exists.  
**Effect on hypothesis:** HYP-002 remains POSSIBLE with LOW confidence until independent corroboration is found.

---

## HYP-003: Target and Account C may represent the same entity or closely related accounts

**Search for contradictory evidence:**

- Display names or usernames that do not match or only loosely correspond
- Different content themes or topics between Target's and Account C's profiles
- Different follower/following communities
- Different geographic or temporal contexts in posts
- Independent evidence connecting Account C to a different person or entity

**Current status:** No contradictory evidence observed yet; visual/username similarity noted but not confirmed.  
**Effect on hypothesis:** HYP-003 remains UNRESOLVED with LOW confidence; per evidence model, visual similarity alone is insufficient for identity confirmation.

---

## HYP-004: The observed interaction patterns are coincidental or based on common public accounts

**Search for contradictory evidence:**

- Repeated direct interactions between Target and specific accounts (supports HYP-001 over HYP-004)
- Shared context beyond common followings (supports meaningful association)
- Interaction patterns that cannot be explained by common followed accounts
- Evidence of one-sided interaction rather than mutual or reciprocal engagement

**Current status:** No contradictory evidence observed yet, but HYP-004 remains as an alternative explanation.  
**Effect on hypothesis:** HYP-004 is maintained as a competing hypothesis to prevent confirmation bias; the investigator should actively seek evidence that distinguishes it from HYP-001.
